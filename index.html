GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

A few things people often want to know before reaching out.

Can you guarantee that my assets will be recovered?+

No. Recovery depends on the specific circumstances, available information, and technical factors. We explain what appears feasible after an initial review, and we never promise a result.

What should I share in the first conversation?+

A high-level description is enough to start. Never send passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or one-time verification codes. We’ll explain what information may be useful and how to share it safely.

What types of digital assets do you focus on?+

Our primary focus is cryptocurrency and digital asset access or transaction concerns. We can discuss your situation and let you know whether it fits our area of work.

Where are you located?+

PromptTech Recovery is based in Manhattan, New York. Initial conversations can be arranged remotely.
